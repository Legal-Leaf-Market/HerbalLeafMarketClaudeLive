What we are showing, and the one thing we need from you

Every other shop we have done this for got a proposed selection. Yours is all 91 of your listings, because your Shopify has no product types set on anything, so there is no honest way for us to sort perfume from salve from cleaning spray without guessing. That is not a complaint about your shop, it just means the sorting has to come from you.

Tell us what to take out and we take it out. Anything you sell only in the shop, anything you are winding down, anything that is really a service. We would rather list twenty products you are happy with than ninety you are not.

And one piece of honesty about fit. Most people arriving at our site are looking for something to drink, steep or take: teas, dried herbs, tinctures. You make skincare, salves, soaps, oils and perfume, which is a genuinely different aisle, and your salves and balms are the part that overlaps. We think that overlap is worth having and we would not have built this page otherwise, but you should go in expecting a narrow stream of the right people rather than a flood.

Two things we could not work out on our own

Your shipping. Your flat rate, and the order value where postage becomes free if you offer that. We show shoppers an estimated out-the-door price, and a maker whose rate we do not know is shown honestly as "shown at checkout" instead. We will not put a guessed number next to your name.

A coupon, only if you want one. Entirely optional, and most makers on our shelf advertise nothing. If you do give us a code we test it at your own checkout before it goes on a card, because a struck-through price your checkout then refuses is the worst thing we could show anybody.

Herbal Leaf Market is a price comparison service. We hold no stock and sell nothing. We earn a commission on some links, which never affects how anything is ranked and is never a reason a maker is listed or delisted.
