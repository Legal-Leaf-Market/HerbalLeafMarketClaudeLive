What we are showing, and the one thing we need from you

Every other shop we have done this for got a proposed selection. Yours is all 91 of your listings, because your Shopify has no product types set on anything, so there is no honest way for us to sort perfume from salve from cleaning spray without guessing. That is not a complaint about your shop, it just means the sorting has to come from you.

Tell us what to take out and we take it out. Anything you sell only in the shop, anything you are winding down, anything that is really a service. We would rather list twenty products you are happy with than ninety you are not.

And one piece of honesty about fit. Plenty of people arriving at our site are looking for something to drink, steep or take, and you do not sell that. What you do sell that our shoppers come for is the Goblin Girl side: salves, balms, salts and sprays made with medicinal herbs, which is the same reason somebody reaches for a herbal remedy in the first place, just applied rather than swallowed. Expect a narrower stream of the right people than a shop full of tinctures would get, and expect them to be the right people.

Who is actually behind this

You are the first shop I have done this for, and your door is about twenty minutes from mine, which is not a coincidence.

My name is Jacob and I live in Columbus, Indiana. My background is accounting, and for the last couple of years I have worked at a technology company that builds accounting software, which let me move out of accounting and into technology generally. I have been pointing that at things I actually care about since, and Indiana is near the top of the list. The other thing I do is busk around Bloomington and Columbus, playing guitar and singing, and my daughter joins me at times on cello.

Herbal Leaf Market started as nothing more than an interest in alternative smoking blends. It turned into a site, and then into three of them, and it found an audience in the wellness community that I genuinely did not see coming. That is roughly where things stand: some early momentum, and a lot of it still being worked out in public.

Which is why this is not a sales pitch. There is nothing to buy here and no plan or tier behind the free one. Everything I have listed so far has been online retailers, and what I have been trying to work out is how to connect the same thing to real shops with real doors. I would rather do that as a partner, early, with people who are still small enough that it can be built around what they actually need.

So if something on this page is wrong, or the whole idea is wrong, tell me. I would honestly rather hear that than not hear back.
