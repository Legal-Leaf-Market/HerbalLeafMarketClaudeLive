# Herbal Leaf Market — crawl policy
#
# /api/ is disallowed because it is not content: /api/inventory is a live
# aggregation of five vendors' catalogues (cached 6h; an uncached crawl hit
# costs a real scrape of every maker's Shopify feed), and /api/rpc is the
# storefront's function endpoint. Nothing there is worth indexing and
# crawling it spends other people's servers.
#
# /admin.html is the owner's console; it already carries noindex, this is
# the second lock.
#
# /facts.html and /stories.html are bare redirect stubs kept so old
# ?page= links resolve; the real pages are in the sitemap.

User-agent: *
Disallow: /api/
Disallow: /admin.html
Disallow: /facts.html
Disallow: /stories.html

Sitemap: https://herballeafmarket.com/sitemap.xml
