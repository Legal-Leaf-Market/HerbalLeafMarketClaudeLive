Privacy

What we keep, and where

Written in plain English by the people who built the site, so it can be precise about what the site actually does. Last updated 8 August 2026.

I

The short version

We set no cookies of our own and we sell nothing about you to anybody. Browsing, filtering and filling a cart all happen inside your own browser. The one time your details reach our server is when you type an email address into a form and press the button: that is what the Garden is, and it is described below, honestly.

We do count what the site is doing, and we say so rather than burying it: which pages get opened, what gets searched for, what gets clicked through to a maker. That count is tied to a number that lives in your tab and dies with it, and it holds no cookie, no IP address and no name. Section III says exactly what is in it.

II

What stays on your device

The site keeps a few things in your browser's local storage so it can remember you between visits. These are not cookies, and they are not sent to us:

  • your cart, and the sizes you picked
  • your Garden hearts (the wishlist), if you use it without signing up
  • the name and email you gave us, so the site can greet you and pre-fill forms
  • that you confirmed you are 21 or older
  • your answers to the Ritual builder's feedback prompt

Clearing site data for this domain in your browser wipes all of it. Nothing breaks; the site simply forgets you.

III

What lands on our server

Your Garden

If you create a Garden or track an item, we store your email address, the name you gave (if any), the items you saved, whether you ticked the consent box, and whether you have unsubscribed. That record exists so we can send the emails you asked for: a welcome note, tracking confirmations, and a weekly recap of your saved items. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing sticks.

Click counts

When you click through to a maker we record the click: which product, which maker, the price shown, the page and filters you were on, and whether you were on a phone. If you are signed in, the record includes your email. We use this to learn which makers and products our visitors actually want, and to show makers honest referral numbers. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

What the site is doing

We also keep a second, deliberately thinner record of how the site itself is used: pages opened, cards opened, searches typed, things added to a cart, and hand-offs to a maker. We read it to find out where the site is failing people, which is a thing we cannot see any other way. The searches that come back empty are the most useful part of it, because that is a list of what you came here for and we did not have.

To make that readable we have to be able to tell one visit's steps from another's, so each visit gets a random number kept in your browser's tab memory. It is thrown away the moment you close the tab, it is never sent anywhere but here, and it cannot follow you between visits or across devices. That record carries no cookie, no IP address, no email address and nothing about your device, and it is deleted after 120 days. It is the least we could keep and still answer the question.

V

Clicking through to a maker

Every product here belongs to an independent maker, and buying happens on their site under their privacy policy, not ours. Links usually carry a referral tag (an Awin or impact.com link, or a code like ref or tr in the address) so the maker knows the visit came from us and any commission is credited. The tag does not tell them who you are, because we do not know either.

VI

Your data, and how to get rid of it

Email hello@herballeafmarket.com and ask. You can ask what we hold on you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. We will delete the record without requiring a reason. For most people the honest answer will be: an email address, a list of saved botanicals, and it is gone now.

This site is for adults 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 21, and if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

If we change how any of this works, we will change this page and move the date at the top. We will not quietly widen what we collect and leave the old page up.

Herbal Leaf Market is an affiliate storefront: we hold no stock, and purchases happen on each maker's own site. This page is a plain description of how the site behaves, written by the people who built it. See also Terms. Adults 21+ only.
