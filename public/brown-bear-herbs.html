Why this maker gets its own page

Most of this shelf is cannabinoids. Brown Bear Herbs is not, and that is exactly why they are here. A shopper who has decided to stop smoking tobacco, or who wants the shape of a cigarette without anything active in it, has almost nowhere sensible to look. The category is small, most of it is sold badly, and a grid of product cards cannot explain the difference between a smoking blend, a tea and a flower essence when the same plants turn up in all three.

So they get a page rather than a row.

What they make

Four different things, and the difference matters more than the shared ingredient list suggests.

Herbal cigarettes

Hand-rolled and filtered, tobacco-free and nicotine-free. The familiar format, without the thing that makes it habit forming.

Loose smoking blends

The same plants sold by weight, to roll yourself or to blend with something else. Cheaper per gram, and you control the mix.

Teas

Many of the same herbs, steeped instead of burned. Worth knowing that a plant can appear on this shelf in both forms and behave differently.

Tinctures and flower essences

Extracts, including essences made to order. This is the part of their range that is dispensed rather than picked off a shelf.

They also do herbal consultations, which is not a product and not something we list. If you want one, that conversation belongs with them directly.

What "nothing in it" actually means

No nicotine. No tobacco. No THC and no CBD. Read plainly, a Brown Bear Herbs cigarette is dried plant matter in a paper, and that is the whole claim.

Smoke is smoke. Burning any plant makes tar and carbon monoxide, and nothing on this page says otherwise.

We say that because the alternative is letting "herbal" do work it cannot do. What is true is narrower and still useful: their blends contain nothing that produces physical dependence, and nothing scheduled. What you do with that is your business, and any question about quitting tobacco belongs with a doctor rather than with a storefront.

Brown Bear Herbs describes their own work in stronger terms than we do, including support for people leaving tobacco behind. That is their framing of their own products and worth reading in their words on their site. This page deliberately does not repeat health claims we are not in a position to stand behind.

The shop

A bricks and mortar apothecary in Portland, not a brand that exists only as a storefront. They keep shop hours, answer a phone, and make the things they sell.
