What this site is, and is not

The plain-English version, which is also the only version. Last updated 8 August 2026.

I

We are a storefront, not a store

Herbal Leaf Market gathers botanical products from independent makers we chose on purpose. We hold no stock, take no payment, and ship nothing. When you check out, you do it on the maker's own website, under their prices, their shipping, their returns policy and their terms. Our cart exists to organise your basket by maker and hand you off cleanly; the sale is always between you and them.

We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases made through our links, at no extra cost to you. That commission is how the site pays for itself, and it never changes which products we show you.

II

Prices are the maker's, and can lag

For most makers we refresh prices and stock from their own catalogues through the day. For some, prices are updated periodically instead, and a maker can change a price or sell out between our last refresh and your click. The price on the maker's checkout page is the real one. If you ever spot a meaningful gap, we would genuinely like to know: hello@herballeafmarket.com.

Discount codes shown on our cards belong to the makers and follow their rules; a maker can retire or change a code at any time.

III

Age, health, and what we do not claim

21+ only. Everything on this site is intended for adults 21 years of age or older.

Product descriptions come from the makers. Statements on this site have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration, and nothing here is intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Nothing on this site is medical advice; talk to a clinician before adding any botanical to your routine, especially alongside medication or during pregnancy.

Combustion of any plant material produces smoke containing chemicals known to the State of California to cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm.

IV

Your Garden

The Garden is a free way to save items and get email about them: a welcome note, tracking confirmations, and a weekly recap. Signing up is never required to browse or to check out. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and what we store is described in the privacy page.

V

The site as it stands

We work to keep the catalogue live and honest, and the site is offered as it stands, without warranties. We are responsible for this website; the makers are responsible for their products, their sites and their fulfilment. Where a dispute concerns an order, it belongs with the maker you bought from, though we will happily point you to the right door.

If we change these terms, we will change this page and move the date at the top.
